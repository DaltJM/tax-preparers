## Website Analysis: taxpreparers.com.au

**Score: 3/10** — A practice with a perfect 5.0 from 29 Google reviews is represented by a site that hides its phone number, leaves template settings on show, and takes several seconds to load on a phone.

![Desktop](screenshot-desktop.png)
![Mobile](screenshot-mobile.png)

### What's Costing You Customers
- **People on phones can't see who you are or how to call.** On a phone the logo is cut off at the edge of the screen and the first screen shows part of a photo and a menu bar, with no phone number or "call us" button. Most people looking for a tax agent are on their phone and will tap the next accountant in the list.
- **Your clients rave about Umang, and the site never mentions him.** Almost every Google review names him and praises how he explains things and rings back. The site says "our team" and shows a stock photo. The trust you have earned is not on the page.
- **The site looks unfinished and unsecured.** Google sends visitors to the "Not secure" version of the address, a "Select your style / Choose your layout" settings panel from the template is visible on every page, and the email address in the footer is a broken link. For a business people trust with their tax file number, those are costly first impressions.

### What We'd Fix (in priority order)
1. Put the phone number, a call button, and the Mill Park address at the top of every page on a phone · _Small project_
2. Show real proof: Google reviews, the Tax Practitioners Board registration, and who the tax agent is · _Small project_
3. Explain each service in plain words with one clear next step (call, email, or enquiry form) · _Small project_
4. Send every visitor to the secure address and fix the broken email link · _Quick win_
5. Make the site load quickly on a phone (it currently loads about 100 files and a 2.3 MB banner image) · _Larger project_ (best done as a rebuild)

### What Caught Our Eye
- **"We Prepare | You Save".** A five-word promise that says exactly what a client gets. It deserves to be the headline, not just the browser tab title.
- **The service lists are specific.** Individual, company, trust, partnership and SMSF returns, BAS, ASIC secretarial work, due diligence for people buying a business. A visitor can tell in seconds whether you handle their situation.
- **Book-keeping benchmarked against ATO industry figures** for hotels, cafes, building and health clinics. That is a concrete reason for a small business owner to choose you over a generic bookkeeper.
- **Two offices, Mill Park and Doreen,** with the Tax Practitioners Board registered badge on the About page. Real local presence and a real credential.

### Technical Details (internal — do NOT send to client)

Method: loaded in Playwright (Chromium) on 1 October 2026 at 1280x800 and 375x812; `/impeccable audit` and `/impeccable critique` were not run as separate commands, the diagnosis below was done by hand from the live page. No PRODUCT.md existed yet (written at Step 2 of the concept workflow).

- Platform: WordPress 4.7.29, "finance" theme with designthemes-core-features plugin, Contact Form 7. Theme style-picker demo panel is live in the DOM (H2 "SELECT YOUR STYLE", 18 picker images).
- HTTPS: `https://www.taxpreparers.com.au/` returns 200 with a valid certificate, but HTTP does not redirect to it; canonical, schema URLs, internal links and the Google listing all use `http://`.
- Performance (375 px, uncached, lab): 98 requests, load event 7.6 s, 33 external scripts. Hero slide `slider-text-1.png` is 2.28 MB (1536x1024 PNG).
- Headings: homepage has no H1; first headings are the style-picker's. Inner pages have an H1.
- Mobile: logo overflows the viewport at 375 px (cropped at right edge); 12 tap targets under 24 px.
- Contact: one `tel:` link on desktop (two at 375 px), footer email is `href="admin@taxpreparers.com.au"` with no `mailto:` so it resolves to a 404 path. Contact form on /contacts/ only (name, email, phone, subject, message).
- SEO: title "We Prepare | You Save - TaxPreparers"; meta description present but cut short ("We work with businesses and individuals to provide them tax advice"); no og:image; schema is WebSite + Organization only, no LocalBusiness/AccountingService; robots.txt is WordPress default; /sitemap.xml 404.
- Images: three homepage images have no alt attribute; hero uses a stock photo of a person.
- Copy issues: "Our Umberlla of Services" (services page), "within a stimulated time frame" (book-keeping), carousel on homepage repeats the same five service blurbs three times in the DOM, service pages show only the Doreen address in the contact strip.
- Fonts: Poppins (theme-loaded).
- 1 console error on load.

Finding-to-command mapping: `/adapt` (mobile header), `/clarify` (CTAs, service copy), `/optimize` (payload), `/distill` (remove picker, repeated carousel), `/harden` (broken mailto, HTTP).
