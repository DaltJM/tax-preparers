Common questions

Are you a registered tax agent?

Yes. TaxPreparers is registered with the Tax Practitioners board to provide Taxation Services to individuals and businesses. Our tax agent number is 25492655.

What tax returns do you prepare?

Individual, partnership, company and trust tax returns, and self managed super fund (SMSF) returns.

Do you work with businesses?

Yes. We provide Business Activity Statements, book-keeping, ASIC secretarial services, and business consulting and due diligence, for small and medium size businesses.
