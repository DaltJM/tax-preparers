## Conflicts between sources

1. **Mill Park address format.** Site: "Suite 6, Level 1, 314-360 Childs Road". Google: "Ste 6, 314/360 Childs Rd". Same place, different wording; owner to choose.
2. **Which office is primary.** Service pages show only the Doreen address in the contact strip; the homepage and Google listing lead with Mill Park. Google has no Doreen listing that I found.
3. **Two ABNs** under the one trading name, one per office. Need to know which entity the site represents.
4. **Prices on the homepage slider (the $120 figure is now shown on the concept at the consultancy's direction, pending owner confirmation).** One slide says "Tax Return from $120"; another says "Tax Compliant Book-Keeping for a reasonable 19/ Week" (no dollar sign). Both need confirming as current before reuse.
5. **Who does the work.** The site speaks of "our team", "qualified staff" and "Tax Wizards" and names nobody. Nearly every Google review names "Umang" (two say "Umang Rathod") as the tax agent.
6. **Website address.** Google links to `http://`; the site also works on `https://` but does not redirect.
7. **Name spelling.** "TaxPreparers" (site) vs "Tax Preparers" (Google, reviews).

## Gaps

- Opening hours. Google showed only "Open · Closes 8 pm" on a Thursday; the full week was not visible and the site lists none.
- Hours and staffing of the Doreen office, and whether it takes walk-ins.
- The principal's name, qualifications and photo. "Umang Rathod" comes only from reviews; do not publish it without the owner's approval.
- Tax agent number 25492655 has not been checked against the TPB public register.
- ASIC registered agent number.
- Year established (a reviewer mentions "over 5+ years"; the site was built in 2016; neither is a stated fact).
- No real photos of the people or the offices. The site's only people photo is a stock image; it will not be reused.
- Service area beyond the two office suburbs.
- Whether appointments can be booked online (a reviewer mentions booking easily; the site has no booking link).
- Whether the "loan related support" a reviewer mentions is an offered service.
- Typos in the business's wording to confirm rather than silently fix: "Umberlla", "stimulated time frame", "Our staffs", "customer service orientated".
