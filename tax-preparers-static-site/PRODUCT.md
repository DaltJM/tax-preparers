# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS with minimal JavaScript, no framework or CMS, per the consultancy's static-site rules. This is a prospect concept: it is never deployed by the concept workflow and stays `noindex`.

## Users

Confirmed with the consultancy on 1 October 2026 (not yet with the business owner):

1. **Individuals who need a tax return done**, usually on a phone, comparing a few local tax agents and wanting to know the price, who they will deal with, and how to call or book. This audience leads.
2. **Small business owners** in Melbourne's north who need BAS, book-keeping, company, trust or partnership returns, ASIC secretarial work, or advice before buying a business. This audience comes second.

## Product Purpose

A website for TaxPreparers, a registered tax agent practice with offices in Mill Park and Doreen, that turns local visitors into phone calls, emails and enquiries. Success is a visitor contacting the practice, not time on site.

## Positioning

- Tagline in the business's own words: "We Prepare | You Save".
- Registered with the Tax Practitioners Board; ASIC registered agents (both stated on the current site).
- Google rating 5.0 from 29 reviews (1 October 2026), in which clients repeatedly praise one named tax agent for explaining things clearly and ringing back.
- Book-keeping compared against ATO industry benchmarks (hotels, cafes, building and construction, health clinics).

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive mostly from Google's local results and the Google listing, which currently links to the `http://` address.
- The primary contact action is a phone call to 0469 348 170; email is admin@taxpreparers.com.au; the current site has an enquiry form on its contact page.
- Mill Park office leads (Stables Shopping Centre, Suite 6, Level 1, 314-360 Childs Road, Mill Park VIC 3082). Doreen office (21 Innovation Road, Doreen VIC 3754) is shown second, pending owner confirmation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services stated by the business: Taxation (individual, partnership, company, trust and SMSF returns), Book-keeping, BAS, ASIC secretarial services, Business Consulting & Due Diligence; plus capital gains advice, estate planning, trust and company formation.
- The concept has no working contact form, analytics or hosting; those belong to the full version.
- State facts only from `VERIFICATION.md`. Anything else is concept copy and is listed in the README for the owner to approve.
- Undecided, do not assert: opening hours; the principal's name, qualifications and photo (the name "Umang Rathod" appears only in reviews); current prices ("Tax Return from $120" and "19/ Week" book-keeping appear on the current slider); which ABN the site represents (two are shown, one per office); whether online booking exists; year established.

## Brand Commitments

- Name as the business writes it: "TaxPreparers". Google lists it as "Tax Preparers - Tax Agents & Accountants".
- Existing logo: dark navy italic wordmark with a swoosh (`original-images/logo.png` (in the client's source-assets folder, kept outside this repository)). Colours come from this logo.
- The business's own wording is kept word for word where used; typos ("Umberlla", "stimulated time frame", "Our staffs") are flagged for the owner, not silently fixed.
- The business calls its people "Tax Wizards".

## Evidence on Hand

- Page wording from all eight live pages: `docs/research/audit/pages-text.txt`.
- Five selected Google reviews, word for word: `docs/research/audit/google-reviews.md`.
- Tax Practitioners Board registered badge image with agent number 25492655 (`original-images/taxagent.jpg` (in the client's source-assets folder, kept outside this repository)); not yet checked against the TPB register.
- Logos of MYOB, Sage and Xero appear on the current contact page.
- No real photographs of people or premises exist. The current site's people photo is stock and must not be reused; no AI-generated people or premises.

## Product Principles

1. The phone number and the next step are never more than a glance away, especially on a phone.
2. Proof comes from real sources only: the registration, the reviews, the business's own words.
3. Plain language for people who find tax stressful; each service says who it is for and what happens first.
4. Unverified facts are omitted or shown as labelled placeholders, never guessed.
5. Individuals first, small businesses second, on every page where both appear.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 Level AA: keyboard operable, visible focus, reflow at 320 px and 200% zoom, 24 px minimum touch targets, reduced-motion support.
