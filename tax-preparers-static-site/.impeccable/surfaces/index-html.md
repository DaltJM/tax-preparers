# Surface brief: home page (and the shared shell for /taxation/ and /contacts/)

Scope: prospect concept for TaxPreparers. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: individuals needing a tax return first, small business owners second, mostly on a phone, deciding who to ring. Action: call 0469 348 170, or email. Proof: Tax Practitioners Board registration, 5.0 from 29 Google reviews, ASIC registered agent, the business's own wording. Constraints: facts only from VERIFICATION.md; no stock or AI people; Iconoir only; self-hosted assets; live URL paths kept.

Direction was pinned by the user at Checkpoint 2 on 1 October 2026 (palette from the logo, IBM Plex Sans, layout); the concept-seed roll (key 0dbda512) was run and set aside for that reason.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the page is the practice's own promise, "We Prepare / You Save", set large in the slanted voice of its logo, with the phone number always one tap away. It refuses the accountant-category default of a stock-photo slider over three icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: logo navy #2F3A82 as whole fields (hero, page heads), deeper navy #1B2152 for the close, warm paper #FAFAF7, navy tint #E9EBF5 bands; one amber #F0B23A reserved for the call action and the hero stroke. IBM Plex Sans, bold italic for display, tabular numerals. Ruled ledger rows instead of cards; flat, no shadows.

STORY: the visitor sees what the practice does, that it is registered and well reviewed, which of the two audiences they are, where the offices are, and rings.

FIRST VIEWPORT: navy field. Left: two-line italic headline up to 6rem with an amber hand-drawn stroke beneath, the business's one-sentence description, amber "Call 0469 348 170" button and a ghost "Email us". Right (below on phones): a ruled list of the four return types linking to /taxation/. Sticky white header with logo and the call button.

FORM: brief-pinned direction, not a rolled candidate; seed key 0dbda512. Signature motion: the amber stroke draws once on load; nothing else animates.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
